# Fish Out of Water

You're a fish on dry land. Flop back to the sea before you dry out.

Hold to curl, let go to flop where you're looking; the arc shows where you'll land. Puddles fill your gills. Let go
just as you touch down for a slap that throws you further. Race seven other fish through the Fish Market, the Kitchen, the
Beach and the Park, chase medals, golden scales and the shared course records in Time Trials, or play the Daily Catch.
Every run feeds the Catch: levels, goals and streaks that unlock fish, colours, hats, trails and splashes in the locker.

Play: https://onceworlds.com/play/fish-out-of-water

## Working on it

- `npm install`, then `npm run build` (Vite, into `dist/`)
- `npm test`: the physics, the arc preview, every course with bots (each golden scale reachable), hazards, cups, ghosts and
  the host's checks
- `?poster=cover` (or `action`, `win`, `multi`, `icon`) renders a store shot of the built game
- `DESIGN.md`: how it plays and why
