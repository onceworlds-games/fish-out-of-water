# Fish Out of Water

You're a fish on dry land. Flop back to the sea before you dry out.

Hold to curl, let go to flop where you're looking; the arc shows where you'll land. Puddles fill your gills. Race seven other
fish through the Fish Market, the Kitchen, the Beach and the Park, or chase medals in Time Trials.

Play: https://onceworlds.com/play/fish-out-of-water

## Working on it

- `npm install`, then `npm run build` (Vite, into `dist/`)
- `npm test`: the physics, the arc preview, every course with bots, hazards, cups, ghosts and the host's checks
- `DESIGN.md`: how it plays and why
